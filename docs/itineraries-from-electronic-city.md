## 1. Heritage, Temples & Tiffin

A Basavanagudi and Lalbagh day that mixes a classic breakfast, a giant Ganesha, billion-year-old rocks, the famous MTR lunch, Tipu's palace and an evening garden walk.

- **Duration:** Full Day (8-9 Hours)
- **On the road:** about 52.3 km and 3 h 05 min in total, including the trip home
- **Budget per person:** about Rs. 1,200
- **Mix:** 4 kinds of places (Cafes & Restaurants, Monuments, Nature, Religious Places)

1. **Taaza Thindi**: Cafes & Restaurants (place #70)
   - Getting there: Cab / auto from Electronic City, leave by 7 AM (~17 km, ~50 min from Electronic City)
   - Self-service idli, vada and filter coffee. Breakfast hours are 7 AM-12 PM; expect a queue on weekends.
2. **Sri Dodda Ganapathi Temple**: Religious Places (place #41)
   - Getting there: Cab (~4 km, ~15 min from the previous stop)
   - 18 ft monolithic Ganesha, free darshan. Open 6:30 AM-12:30 PM and 5:30-8:30 PM.
3. **Bugle Rock Park** (hidden gem): Nature (place #52)
   - Getting there: Walk (next to the temple) (~0.3 km, ~5 min from the previous stop)
   - Hidden gem. Ancient granite rocks and a Kempegowda watchtower. Wear comfortable shoes; allow 45 min.
4. **MTR**: Cafes & Restaurants (place #74)
   - Getting there: Auto (~4 km, ~20 min from the previous stop)
   - Rava idli and the unlimited lunch thali, around Rs. 500. Closed on Mondays, lunch is 12:30-2:30 PM.
5. **Tipu Sultan's Summer Palace**: Monuments (place #49)
   - Getting there: Auto (~4 km, ~20 min from the previous stop)
   - Wooden palace and small museum, about Rs. 20 entry. Open 8:30 AM-5:30 PM.
6. **Lalbagh Botanical Garden**: Nature (place #7)
   - Getting there: Auto (~3 km, ~15 min from the previous stop)
   - Finish with a slow walk through 240 acres of garden. Small entry fee; best 5:30-7 PM.
- Back to Electronic City: ~20 km, ~60 min
